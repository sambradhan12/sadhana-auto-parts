Welcome to Sadhana Auto Parts

Your trusted partner for quality automotive solutions.

Located in the heart of Parsa, Chitwan, Sadhana Auto Parts is a dedicated provider of high-performance automobile components. We supply a wide range of essential parts—including engines, wheels, and accessories—specially tailored for two-wheelers such as bikes, scooters, motorcycles, and bicycles.

What sets us apart is our commitment to quality, affordability, and customer trust. Whether you're a vehicle owner, local mechanic, or workshop, we deliver durable products that keep your machines running smoothly.

Our long-term vision is bold: to transform Sadhana Auto Parts into a globally recognized brand, starting from Nepal and expanding into international markets. With a foundation built on integrity, innovation, and customer-first service, we aim to lead the future of auto parts distribution across borders.
